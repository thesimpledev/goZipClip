Privacy Statement

ZipClip works on your computer, for you. Nothing about you is collected, and nothing is sent anywhere except the requests needed to download your own videos.

Effective date: August 12, 2026

The short version

ZipClip does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no cloud services. The app's only network activity is connecting to Twitch to check for and download VODs from the channel you configure, and, only if you turn on automatic uploads, connecting to YouTube to upload your finished videos to your own channel.

What the app stores on your computer

  • Your videos. Downloaded VODs, the trimmed results, and your intro clip live in folders on your own machine that you choose. Intermediate working files are deleted automatically when processing finishes.
  • Settings and history. Your configuration (channel, folders, schedule) and the list of VODs already processed are stored in local files so the app knows what it has already done. They never leave your computer.

What the app never does

  • It never connects to anything other than Twitch, to download your VODs, and YouTube, only when you have turned automatic uploads on.
  • It never sends your videos, settings, or any other information anywhere else. With automatic uploads on, your finished videos go only to your own YouTube channel, as private videos.
  • It never touches your YouTube account beyond uploading. The permission you grant covers uploads only, and you can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. With uploads set to manual, it never touches your YouTube account at all.
  • It never runs analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting of its own.
  • It never shows ads or uses third-party tracking of any kind.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics of our own. It is hosted on Amazon Web Services (S3 and CloudFront), which may log standard server information such as visitor IP addresses.

Children

ZipClip collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this statement

If this statement ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
