Your VODs, Trimmed and Ready to Upload

ZipClip watches for your new Twitch VODs, cuts off the starting-soon screen, splices in your intro, and hands the finished video to your upload tool. Once a day, all by itself.

Download Alpha 0.1.0 out now for Windows and Linux.

Features

📥

Automatic Downloads

Checks for new VODs on your schedule and downloads them, skipping anything already processed.

🔍

Finds the Real Start

Detects the moment your waiting screen switches to the actual stream. No manual scrubbing.

✂️

Cuts Without Re-encoding

Trims the prestream section and splices your intro onto the front. No quality loss, no hours-long encodes.

📁

Watch Folder Handoff

Drops the finished file into the folder your upload software already watches. Your existing workflow stays the same.

🧹

Cleans Up After Itself

Deletes intermediate files once a VOD is finished, so your disk space stays yours.

How It Works

1

Download. Once a day, ZipClip grabs any new VODs from your Twitch channel.

2

Detect. It scans the video for the jump from your starting-soon screen to the real stream.

3

Trim and splice. The prestream wait is cut off and your pre-made intro is stitched onto the front, with no re-encode.

4

Deliver. The finished video lands in your upload tool's watch folder, ready to go to YouTube.

5

Clean up. Temporary files are deleted so the only thing left is the video you wanted.
